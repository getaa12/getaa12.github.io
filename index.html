Block Ads System-Wide
Free · No app needed · Works everywhere
Use AdGuard DNS to block ads, trackers and malware at the network level — before they even load. Just change one setting on your device, no installs needed.
AdGuard DNS Address
dns.adguard-dns.com
Setup on Android (Android 9+)
1
Open Settings on your Android phone
2
Go to Network & Internet → Advanced → Private DNS
3
Select Private DNS provider hostname
4
Type: dns.adguard-dns.com then tap Save
✓
Done! Ads will be blocked across all apps & browsers.
📺 Video Tutorial
💡 On some phones the path may differ slightly — search "Private DNS" in Settings
Setup on iPhone / iPad (iOS 14+)
1
Open this link in Safari on your iPhone to download the AdGuard DNS config profile:
adguard-dns.io → Download Profile
2
Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
3
Tap the AdGuard DNS profile and hit Install
4
Confirm by entering your passcode when prompted
✓
Done! Ads blocked in Safari, apps, and all browsers.
📺 Video Tutorial
💡 To remove: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Delete Profile
Setup on Windows 10 / 11
1
Open Settings → Network & Internet → Change adapter options
2
Right-click your active connection → Properties
3
Select Internet Protocol Version 4 (TCP/IPv4) → Properties
4
Choose Use the following DNS server addresses and enter:
Primary:   94.140.14.14
Secondary: 94.140.15.15
5
Click OK to save. That's it!
✓
Done! Ads blocked across all browsers and apps on Windows.

💡 Windows 11 also supports DNS-over-HTTPS — search "DNS encryption" in Settings for extra privacy
